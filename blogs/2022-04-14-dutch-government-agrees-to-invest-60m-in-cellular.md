We are excited to share that the Dutch government announced today it will be earmarking € 60 million to support the formation of an ecosystem around cellular agriculture. This represents the largest public funding into the cellular agriculture field ever, globally. The funding represents a first step towards a larger growth plan proposing to invest € 252–€ 382 million in cellular agriculture as part of the National Growth Fund , which aims to create structural economic growth by investing in innovative economic sectors.
