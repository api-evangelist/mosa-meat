Today, we’re celebrating taking the next step in the scaling up of our facilities at Mosa Meat for the production of cultivated beef. We’re expanding to a total of 7,340 m2 facilities (77,000 sq ft.), making it the largest cultivated meat campus in the world. Our new industrial production development centre is just a few minutes down the street from our existing pilot facility in Maastricht.
