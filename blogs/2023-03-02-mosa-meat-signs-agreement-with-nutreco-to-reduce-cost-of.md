Today, we are pleased to share that we have signed an agreement with our partner and investor Nutreco to collaborate on creating a cell feed supply chain. This is a new milestone in our longstanding partnership. In October 2021, we were jointly awarded a React EU grant * for the ‘Feed for Meat’ project, which strives to lower the cost of cultivated beef while creating a robust supply chain to scale up production.
