We are very pleased to announce we are welcoming new and existing partners to help bring cultivated beef to consumers, as part of €40M in new capital we raised today to finance our next phase. The funds will be used to further scale up our production processes, drive down the cost of production and prepare for market entry. The oversubscribed round was led by Lowercarbon Capital and M Ventures .
