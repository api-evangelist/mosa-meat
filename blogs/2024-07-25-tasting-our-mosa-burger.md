We’re excited to share that we’ve organised the first formal tasting of our Mosa Burger in The Netherlands. It’s the first time a cultivated beef product was tasted in the EU after a ‘ code of practice ’ was created in 2023, that makes tastings of cultivated foods under controlled conditions possible before receiving an EU-wide approval for sales. Participants included Dutch cattle farmers, food product developers and industry representatives who shared their candid tasting feedback with us.
