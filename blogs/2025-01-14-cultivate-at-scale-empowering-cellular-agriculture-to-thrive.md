At Mosa Meat, we're proud to share an exciting milestone in the evolution of cellular agriculture. Over the years, we’ve pushed the boundaries of what’s possible with cultivated meat. Now, we're taking the next step to empower the broader cellular agriculture ecosystem: the graduation of our facility into Cultivate at Scale, an independent, open-access scale-up center for cell culture innovation.
